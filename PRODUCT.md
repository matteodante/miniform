# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Miniform serves small technical teams and self-hosting operators who need to collect form responses without sending customer data through a managed form service. They work in a private operator console to inspect entries, configure endpoints, manage delivery routes, and verify safeguards with minimal operational overhead.

## Product Purpose

Miniform provides a quiet, private inbox for HTML form responses. It keeps submissions and files on the operator's server, forwards accepted entries through reliable email or webhook delivery, and makes delivery state understandable. Success means an operator can configure collection safely, find a response quickly, and understand what happened without depending on Miniform-operated infrastructure.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, direct, practical, and protective. The product should feel calm and focused without becoming vague; concise without becoming cryptic; operational rather than promotional; and confident about data ownership without using alarmist language.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, promotional admin surfaces, ornamental data visualizations, decorative motion, excessive cards, and interfaces that hide operational state behind marketing language. Do not use hype, filler illustrations, or fashionable effects that compete with the operator's task.

## Design Principles

- Keep the operator in the task: entries, endpoints, delivery, and safeguards remain easy to locate and understand.
- Make system state explicit: show what happened, when it happened, and what the operator can do next.
- Practice data ownership: reinforce privacy and self-hosting through concrete behavior rather than promotional claims.
- Prefer calm precision: use direct language, familiar controls, and a consistent interaction vocabulary.
- Teach through empty and error states: every interruption should offer a clear recovery or next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient text and control contrast, and status communication that does not depend on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep core workflows usable without decorative animation.
